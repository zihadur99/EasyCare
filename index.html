FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?+

Both are welcome. Walk-ins are seen as soon as a chair is free, but calling ahead on 01715-886152 means less waiting, especially in the evenings.

How much does treatment cost?+

Prices depend on what you need, so we always examine first and tell you the cost before starting anything. No surprises. Placeholder: add a starting price for check-ups, scaling and fillings here if you'd like.

What should I do in a dental emergency?+

For severe toothache, swelling, a broken tooth or bleeding after an extraction, call us right away on 01715-886152. We'll fit you in as soon as possible during opening hours (until 9 PM daily). For heavy bleeding or facial swelling affecting breathing, go to the nearest hospital.

I'm very nervous. Will it hurt?+

Tell us when you arrive. We'll explain each step, use numbing where needed and agree a "stop" signal you can use at any time. Most patients are surprised how easy it is.
